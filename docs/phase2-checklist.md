# Phase 2: full cockpit detailing

Every item is matched against the owner's photos of the aircraft and the reference model.
Nothing is copied from the reference model: every part is modelled from scratch.

## A. Fill the cockpit (no naked areas, no sky through gaps)
- [ ] Close every gap where the sky shows through: window post junctions, glareshield ends, overhead edges, roof seams
- [ ] Overhead surround: side fairings on both sides of the overhead panel, the rear (aft) face of the overhead, the ceiling around it
- [ ] Roof / ceiling lining: panels, seams, dome lights, air outlets, loudspeaker, the headset/cable stowage recesses (as photographed)
- [ ] Sidewalls both sides: moulded side-window frames, the sliding-window handle, sun visor rails, escape rope, the console face panels, lower sidewall stowage, the oxygen mask box, the cup holder
- [ ] Rear area: jump seat, circuit breaker panels, extinguisher, document stowage, smoke hood, gloves, landing gear pins box (as photographed)
- [ ] Floor: carpet, footrest areas, the rudder pedal housings with PULL TO ADJUST handles

## B. Panel boundaries and colour grading
- [ ] Every panel a separate plate with its own border, gap and Dzus fasteners at the photographed spots
- [ ] Correct paint per area: overhead panels, main panel, glareshield panels, pedestal panels, console tops, linings
- [ ] Integral lighting: amber outlines and lit legend paint as on the aircraft
- [ ] Panel engraving: titles, vertical side titles (FIRE, HYD, FUEL, ELEC, AIR COND), dividers, green flow lines, boxed labels

## C. Every control to its real type and shape
- [ ] Pushbuttons: correct legend per button (top and bottom windows), with correct colours (FAULT amber, OFF white, ON blue, AVAIL green, FIRE red)
- [ ] Guards: red guarded covers, black cages, the fire pushbutton covers showing FIRE / PUSH
- [ ] Toggles: the lever-lock toggle types (EXT LT strobe/beacon/wing/nav, landing light, nose light, signs) each in its real shape
- [ ] Knobs: overhead bar knobs, temperature knobs, ADIRS knobs, ENG MODE selector, ECAM knobs, RMP frequency knobs, FCU push-pull knobs, EFIS knobs
- [ ] Engine master switches with ENG 1 / ENG 2 caps, FIRE / FAULT lights
- [ ] Levers: thrust levers and gate scale, speed brake, flaps, park brake handle, gravity gear extension handle, gear lever, pitch trim wheels and scale
- [ ] Sidestick with stepped boot, tiller with lit dial, rudder pedals
- [ ] Every legend and number on every panel

## D. Displays
- [ ] PFD, ND, E/WD, SD, ISIS, DCDU, clock, MCDU / FMS screens and keys, RMP and ATC windows, FCU windows, BAT voltage windows, RUD TRIM window
- [ ] Each display at its real position, size and bezel, with correct content
